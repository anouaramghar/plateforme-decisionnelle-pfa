# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
ENIAD faculty (enseignants), filière responsables, and admins using a BI &
predictive-analytics platform to spot and act on at-risk students. They work in
French, often from a desk during/after class, scanning grade trends, risk
scores, and alerts. Some are technical (admins), many are not (teaching staff).
The job: see who's struggling, why, and decide what to do — fast and with trust
in the numbers.

### Brand Personality
Warm & approachable, three words: **trustworthy, human, grounded**. It softens
the institutional/academic feel without becoming toy-like. Data should feel
reliable and calm, never alarming or hype-y. French-language UI.

### Aesthetic Direction
Already established and intentional — keep it, don't reinvent it:
- **Type**: Geist (sans) + Geist Mono + Instrument Serif (italic display).
- **Palette**: warm stone neutrals + amber/orange accent (`--accent-*`), not the
  default BI blue. Status: green/amber/red/sky. OKLCH `color-mix` for tints.
- **Theme**: light + dark (class-based), with a permanently-dark Linear-style
  sidebar in both.
- **Density**: compact / comfortable / spacious tokens via `--pad`.
- **Anti-references**: generic BI-blue dashboards, AI-slop (cyan-on-dark,
  purple gradients, glassmorphism, identical card grids, gradient-text metrics).

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth through neutrals, not decoration** — tint stone/amber, avoid cold gray-on-color.
2. **Calm authority** — restraint over visual noise; the data is the hero, not chrome.
3. **Keyboard-first** — command palette, focus states, and tab order are first-class.
4. **Tokens over hard-codes** — colors/spacing/radius flow from CSS variables.
5. **Accessible by default** — target WCAG 2.1 AA, with strong keyboard operability.
